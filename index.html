Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our event planning process.

How far in advance should I book?

For major weddings and large conferences, we recommend booking at least 15-30 Days in advance. Smaller corporate events can be often arranged within 15-30 Days.

Do you handle events outside of Addis Ababa?

Yes! We coordinate destination weddings and corporate retreats across Ethiopia, including Lalibela, Bahir Dar, and Hawassa.

What is your payment structure?

We typically require a 30% deposit to secure your date, 40% midway through planning, and the final 30% one week before the event.
